Cro-Mag Rally

This is a custom fork of jorio's original Cro-Mag Rally port. See the changelog below for all new additions and base-game bug fixes added in this edition.

Laptop or Desktop Computer Required

Cro-Mag Rally needs a laptop or desktop computer with a keyboard or controller and does not work on phones, tablets, or small screens. Open this page on a computer to race!

  • Drive & Steer Arrow Keys or WASD
  • Throw Weapon Spacebar
  • Menus & Pick Enter or Spacebar
  • Back / Pause Esc

Controllers work too — just plug in and press a button once the race loads.

Local Saves: Progress, settings, and best times are saved right on this device. Clearing your cookies or site data, using a private window, or switching devices will reset your saved progress.

Lineup Settings

Turning Off Paw Patrol

Paw Patrol characters and rescue vehicles start switched on by default. Prefer the original caveman roster?

  1. Open OPTIONS from the main menu.
  2. Switch PAW PATROL to OFF to hide all Paw Patrol drivers and vehicles.
  3. Want to drive them yourself without computer rivals using them? Leave PAW PATROL on and set CPU PAW PATROL to OFF.
